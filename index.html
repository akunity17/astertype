How we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit this site

— Last updated 17.09.2026

General Information

[01]

Who we are

Astertype Studio is a design studio based in Porto, Portugal.

Contact — astertypestudio@gmail.com
If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, reach out at the email above.

[02]

What data we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect what you provide your name, email address, and the content of your message, nothing more.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, which can include your IP address, browser type, device, the pages you visit, and time spent on each.

We don’t collect any special categories of data, such as health, religious, or political information, and we never ask for more than a form needs.

How we use your data

[03]

Why we collect it

We use your data to respond to your enquiries when you contact us through the form, and to understand how the site is used so we can improve it over time.

Seeing which pages get visited and which don’t helps us know where things are working and where they need attention.

[04]

Legal basis for processing

We mainly use Google Analytics to see how visitors move through the site, which pages, for how long, and from where.

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings; this won’t stop you using the site, it just means we won’t see your visit in our analytics.

[05]

Data sharing

We don’t sell, rent, or trade your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, like Google as our analytics provider, and only to the extent their service requires, under their own privacy terms.

We may also share it if legally required to, but never for marketing purposes.

Your rights & security

[06]

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, then delete or anonymize them, unless the law requires otherwise. Analytics data follows our provider’s own retention settings.

[07]

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

[08]

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to

Access the personal data we hold about you; Request correction of inaccurate data; Request deletion of your data; Object to or restrict certain processing; Request a copy of your data in a portable format; Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

[09]

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at astertypestudio@gmail.com.
